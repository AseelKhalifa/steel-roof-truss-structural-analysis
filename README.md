# Structural Analysis of Steel Roof Truss Systems

## Project Overview
A collaborative civil engineering project completed at McMaster University, applying principles of statics and mechanics of materials to analyze a structural roof system consisting of steel trusses, purlins, concrete columns, and supporting corbels.

## Project Objectives
- Evaluate structural load distribution and load transfer.
- Calculate dead loads, snow loads, and support reactions.
- Analyze axial forces, stresses, and strains.
- Determine member deformation under applied loads.
- Develop engineering diagrams and detailed calculations.
